AP Biology Unit 3 Notes: Cellular Respiration

Glycolysis happens in the cytoplasm. One glucose becomes two pyruvate, with a net gain of 2 ATP and 2 NADH.
The Krebs cycle happens in the mitochondrial matrix. Each turn releases CO2 and makes NADH, FADH2, and 1 ATP.
The electron transport chain is in the inner mitochondrial membrane. It pumps protons and drives ATP synthase.
Oxygen is the final electron acceptor and forms water.
Total yield is about 30 to 32 ATP per glucose.
Without oxygen, cells use fermentation: lactic acid in muscles, ethanol in yeast.

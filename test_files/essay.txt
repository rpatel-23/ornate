The Importance of Sleep for Students

Many students dont get enough sleep during the school year. Teenagers needs about 8 to 10 hours of sleep each night,
but most only gets 6 or 7. This effect there grades, mood, and health. When students is tired, they have trouble focusing
in class and remembering what they learned.

Schools could help by starting later in the morning. Some district that tried later start times seen better attendance
and test scores. Students can also help themselfs by putting there phones away an hour before bed.
